# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and welcome people of every background and level of experience. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory or sexualised language or imagery aren't acceptable in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainer. On GitHub, open the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say it's a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that don't follow this code, and may ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, temporarily or permanently.
